# Scol

**A personal tutor for every child in Classes 1 to 4.**

<img src="app/branding/logo.png" width="96" alt="Scol">

Scol is an Android app where a friendly little creature teaches your child their own school
syllabus, one short level at a time. The tutor explains, shows pictures, asks questions, notices
where your child is stuck, and tries again in a different way. It works in English, Malayalam, or both together.

Scol follows two syllabuses: **Kerala State (SCERT)** and **CBSE (NCERT)**.

> Scol is in early testing with a small number of families. The first complete subject is
> **Class 3 Maths, Kerala syllabus**: 24 levels across the six units of Part 1 of the textbook.
> More classes and subjects are being added.

## How a lesson works

1. **Your child opens the app.** Their tutor greets them and has already chosen what to learn today.
   Ideas your child found hard last time come first, then new lessons.
2. **The tutor teaches.** A short explanation in simple words, spoken aloud, with pictures that build
   up on the board: baskets of mangoes for multiplication, an abacus for hundreds, tens and ones,
   a scale for centimetres.
3. **The tutor asks.** Your child answers by tapping a choice or typing a number. A level takes about five minutes.
4. **Hands on.** Some questions are done with fingers: dragging sticks to build a triangle,
   moving beads on an abacus to make 235, sliding a scale under a pencil to measure it.
5. **A wrong answer is never the end.** The tutor does not give the answer away. It gives a hint and
   lets your child try again. If it is still hard, the tutor corrects the mistake kindly and then
   explains the idea another way, as a little story or as a picture, and asks again. Scol remembers
   which way works better for your child.
6. **Stars, not pressure.** Your child earns stars, builds a streak of learning days, and collects badges.
   Missing a day takes nothing away.

## How Scol knows whether your child has understood

Most learning apps count right answers. Scol looks deeper, because a child can get a question right by guessing or by memorising.

For every idea, such as "counting equal groups", Scol:

- asks several questions, not one;
- asks in different ways (pick an answer, type a number, a new situation);
- treats an instant tap as a possible guess, not as proof;
- remembers the kind of mistake, so it can tell "added the numbers instead of making groups" from a slip.

An idea counts as understood only after your child gets it right several times, in more than one
kind of question. Ideas learned a week ago come back for a quick check.

## For parents

Tap **Parents** on the home screen (a small sum keeps children out). You will see:

- **A weekly report** in English or Malayalam: days and minutes learned, levels finished, what is understood,
  what needs practice, and whether stories or pictures help your child more. Share it (for example on WhatsApp)
  or press Listen to hear it read aloud.
- **What your child understands, in plain words.** For example: *"Counting equal groups: keeps
  making the same mistake: added the numbers instead of making groups. Scol will teach this again in a different way."*
- **Time spent today, lessons done, and learning days.**
- **Controls:** tutor language, which tutor, a daily time limit (15 to 60 minutes), and voice on or off.

When the daily limit is reached, the tutor says goodbye until tomorrow.

| **Ruby** (റൂബി) | Always curious. Loves asking why. |
| **Ollie** (ഒല്ലി) | Funny and relaxed. Learning feels like a joke between friends. |

They feel alive. Their eyes follow where they are going, look at the picture while they teach,
look at your child while they talk, and glance at wherever your child touches the screen.
Your child picks one and can switch at any time.

## Your child's privacy

- A parent sets up the app. Scol asks only for a first name or nickname.
- Your child's name and progress are stored **on your phone only**. They are not sent to us or anyone else.
- The tutors' voices are sound files we prepare in advance. Nothing your child says or does is sent to any AI service. The app does not record your child.
- No advertisements.
- You can delete everything with one button in the parent area.

The app uses the internet only to download new lessons and the tutors' voice files.

## Try it

1. On an Android phone or tablet, open the [Releases page](https://github.com/Amaljith75/scol/releases).
2. Download the **.apk** file from the newest release (for example "Scol V1 build 2") and open it to install.
   (Your phone may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser.)
3. Do the one-minute parent setup: name, syllabus, class, language, tutor.
4. Hand the phone to your child.

The tutors speak English and Malayalam in natural voices. The first time a level is opened the voice
files are downloaded, so use Wi-Fi or mobile data for that. After that the level works offline.

## Questions

**Does Scol replace school or the teacher?** No. It gives your child extra one-to-one attention at home, on the same syllabus the school follows.

**How much does it cost?** Free during testing.

**Does it work without internet?** Yes. Lessons already on the phone work offline.

**My child is in a class or subject that shows "Lessons are on the way".** Those chapters are still being prepared. They will appear in the app by themselves.
