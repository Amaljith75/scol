# Clean hands
objective: Wash hands the right way and know when to wash them.
concept: clean-hands
concept.name: Washing hands
minutes: 6

## say
en: Our hands get dirty when we play and work. Dirt and tiny germs on our hands can reach our mouth with food and make us ill.
visual: emoji 🙌 🦠

## say
en: Washing with soap destroys germs. Wash your hands before eating, after eating, after using the toilet, and after travelling.
visual: steps before eating / after eating / after the toilet / after travelling

## say
en: Washing has 6 steps. One: wet your hands and put on soap. Two: rub your palms together. Three: clean the backs of your hands and between your fingers.
visual: steps 1 wet + soap / 2 rub palms / 3 backs and between fingers

## say
en: Four: rub your nails and fingertips on your palm. Five: rub your fingers and wrists in circles. Six: rinse in clean water and dry with a clean cloth.
visual: steps 4 nails, fingertips / 5 fingers, wrists / 6 rinse and dry

## say
label: example
en: Trim and clean your nails too. Dirt hiding under nails can reach your stomach with food.
visual: emoji 💅 ✂️

## ask
form: number
en: How many steps are there in washing hands properly?
answer: 6
hint.en: Count the steps from wetting to drying.
explain.en: There are 6 steps.
mistake 3: remembered only some of the steps

## ask
form: choice
en: When must we wash our hands with soap?
options: after using the toilet | only on Sundays | only when they look black
answer: after using the toilet
hint.en: Think of when germs get on our hands.
explain.en: Wash with soap after using the toilet, and before and after eating.
mistake only when they look black: thinks germs can be seen

## ask
form: choice
en: What does soap do to germs?
options: it destroys them | it feeds them | nothing
answer: it destroys them
hint.en: That is why we use soap and not only water.
explain.en: Soap destroys germs on our hands.

## ask
form: choice
en: Why should we trim our nails?
options: dirt under nails can reach our stomach | to make our hands heavier | nails grow faster when trimmed
answer: dirt under nails can reach our stomach
hint.en: Think of where food goes after it touches our fingers.
explain.en: Dirt under long nails can go into our stomach with food.

## ask
form: choice
en: What is the last step of washing hands?
options: rinse and dry with a clean cloth | put on soap | rub palms together
answer: rinse and dry with a clean cloth
hint.en: It comes after all the rubbing.
explain.en: The last step is to rinse in clean water and dry with a clean cloth.

## story
en: The children washed their hands into one bucket. Then they compared a glass of fresh water with a glass of the washing water. The washing water was cloudy! Alan said, so that is what was on our hands!

## picture
visual: steps 🙌 + 🧼 → 🦠 gone / clean 🙌 → safe 🍛
en: Clean hands keep germs away from our food.
