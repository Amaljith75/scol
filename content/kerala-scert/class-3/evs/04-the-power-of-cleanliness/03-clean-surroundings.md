# Clean surroundings
objective: Keep surroundings clean to stop disease.
concept: clean-surroundings
concept.name: Clean surroundings and disease
minutes: 6

## say
en: Mosquitoes, flies and rats multiply where there is waste and dirty water. They spread many diseases.
visual: emoji 🦟 🪰 🐀

## say
en: Mosquitoes spread dengue fever, malaria, chikungunya and elephantiasis. They lay their eggs in collected water.
visual: emoji 🦟 💧

## say
en: Coconut shells, old tyres, bottles, caps and toys lying outside can collect rainwater. Mosquito eggs in such water become adult mosquitoes in eight days.
visual: emoji 🥥 🛞 🍾

## say
en: So once every seven days, we empty that water and remove things that can hold water. This is called a dry day.
visual: text every 7 days = dry day

## say
en: Some diseases spread through dirty water: dysentery, jaundice, cholera and typhoid. Drink boiled and cooled water.
visual: emoji 🫖 💧

## say
label: example
en: Dysentery makes the body lose water. It needs to be given back: salted rice water, salted buttermilk, coconut water, or lime juice with salt and sugar. ORS from the health centre, mixed in boiled and cooled water, helps too.
visual: emoji 🥥 🍋 🥛

## ask
form: number
en: How often should we have a dry day? Once every how many days?
answer: 7
hint.en: Mosquito eggs take 8 days to become adults, so we clear water a day before.
explain.en: Once every 7 days.
mistake 8: chose the mosquito growth time instead of the dry-day gap

## ask
form: number
en: In how many days do mosquito eggs become adult mosquitoes?
answer: 8
hint.en: It is one day more than a week.
explain.en: In 8 days. That is why dry day comes every 7 days.
mistake 7: mixed up the dry-day gap and the mosquito growth time

## ask
form: choice
en: Which disease is spread by mosquitoes?
options: dengue fever | toothache | a broken arm
answer: dengue fever
hint.en: Mosquitoes spread dengue, malaria and chikungunya.
explain.en: Dengue fever is spread by mosquitoes.

## ask
form: choice
en: Where do mosquitoes lay their eggs?
options: in collected water | in dry sand | in a hot oven
answer: in collected water
hint.en: Think of the coconut shells after rain.
explain.en: Mosquitoes lay their eggs in collected water.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which drink helps someone who has dysentery?
options: salted rice water | a fizzy cold drink | nothing at all
answer: salted rice water
hint.en: The body has lost water and salt.
explain.en: Salted rice water, buttermilk, coconut water or ORS give back lost water.
