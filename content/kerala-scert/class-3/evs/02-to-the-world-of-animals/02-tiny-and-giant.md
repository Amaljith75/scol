# Tiny ones and giants
objective: Notice how animals differ in size, colour, spots, horns and where they live.
concept: animal-variety
concept.name: Variety in animals
minutes: 6

## say
en: Some creatures are tiny, like ants. Some are giants, like elephants. We can arrange animals from the smallest to the largest.
visual: pattern 🐜 🐸 🐕 🐄 🐘

## say
en: Tiny creatures live in the soil and on plants. A hand lens makes them look bigger, so we can see them clearly.
visual: emoji 🔍 🐜 🐛

## say
en: We can recognise animals by their features: colour, spots, stripes, horns and nails. A zebra has stripes. A leopard has spots. A cow has horns.
visual: emoji 🦓 🐆 🐄

## say
en: The sea is home to more creatures than the land! Fish, sea horses, sea cucumbers and sea cows all live there.
visual: emoji 🐠 🐙 🦀

## say
label: example
en: The largest creature on earth is the blue whale. It lives in the sea, but it is not a fish. A blue whale gives birth to its babies.
visual: emoji 🐋

## ask
form: choice
en: Which is the largest creature on earth?
options: blue whale | elephant | shark
answer: blue whale
hint.en: It lives in the sea.
explain.en: The blue whale is the largest creature on earth.
mistake elephant: thinks the largest animal lives on land

## ask
form: choice
en: Is the blue whale a fish?
options: No | Yes
answer: No
hint.en: It gives birth to its babies instead of laying eggs like most fish.
explain.en: No. The blue whale is not a fish. It gives birth to its young.
mistake Yes: thinks everything in the sea is a fish

## ask
form: choice
en: Where do more creatures live?
options: in the sea | on land
answer: in the sea
hint.en: Think of how big the sea is.
explain.en: There are more creatures in the sea than on land.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which animal can you recognise by its stripes?
options: zebra | elephant | frog
answer: zebra
hint.en: Black and white lines.
explain.en: A zebra has black and white stripes.

## ask
form: choice
en: What helps us see tiny creatures clearly?
options: a hand lens | a spoon | a mirror
answer: a hand lens
hint.en: It makes small things look bigger.
explain.en: A hand lens makes tiny creatures look bigger.

## ask
form: number
en: Ant, elephant and blue whale. How many of these live in the sea?
answer: 1
hint.en: Only one of them swims in the sea.
explain.en: Only 1: the blue whale.
mistake 2: thinks the elephant lives in the sea

## story
en: Mithra and her friends played a naming game. Grasshopper! Firefly! Beetle! Cow! Then her brother said, blue whale! Everyone laughed. You win, he said, that is the biggest of all.
