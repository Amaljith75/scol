# Leaves, flowers and why plants matter
objective: Notice the variety in leaves and flowers, and why plants matter.
concept: leaves-flowers
concept.name: Leaves, flowers and the value of plants
minutes: 6

## say
en: Leaves are not all the same. They differ in shape, size and colour, and some leaves have a smell when you crush them. Thumba leaves are long and tapering.
visual: emoji 🍃 🌿 🍁

## say
en: Flowers are different too, in colour, smell and shape. Jasmine is white and sweet-smelling. Flowers give nectar to bees and butterflies, and many flowers later become fruits.
visual: emoji 🌼 🐝 🍎

## say
en: Not every plant has flowers. Ferns and mosses never bloom. And not every flower becomes a fruit.
visual: emoji 🌿

## say
en: Here is a special one. Neelakurinji blooms only once in twelve years. Its flowers are blue and can stay for up to three months. The Nilgiri hills are named after it.
visual: text 12 years 💙

## say
en: Plants give us shade, food, flowers and fresh air, and homes for birds and insects. So it is our duty to plant and protect plants and keep the earth green.
visual: emoji 🌳 🍌 🐦 🌍

## ask
form: number
en: Neelakurinji blooms once in how many years?
answer: 12
hint.en: It is a long wait: one dozen years.
explain.en: Neelakurinji blooms once in twelve years.
mistake 1: thinks it blooms every year

## ask
form: choice
en: What colour are Neelakurinji flowers?
options: blue | red | yellow
answer: blue
hint.en: Neela means blue.
explain.en: Neelakurinji flowers are blue. That is why the Nilgiri hills got their name.

## ask
form: choice
en: Do all plants have flowers?
options: No | Yes
answer: No
hint.en: Think of ferns and mosses.
explain.en: No. Ferns and mosses do not bloom.
mistake Yes: thinks every plant flowers

## ask
form: choice
en: Who gets nectar from flowers?
options: bees | fish | cows
answer: bees
hint.en: This insect makes honey.
explain.en: Bees and butterflies drink nectar from flowers.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which of these is a way plants help us?
options: They give shade | They make noise | They eat plastic
answer: They give shade
hint.en: Think of sitting under a big tree on a hot day.
explain.en: Plants give shade, food, fresh air and homes for animals.

## story
en: A little flower on a hibiscus plant said, please don't pluck me! A bee is coming to drink my nectar, and soon I will become a seed pod, so more hibiscus plants can grow.

## picture
visual: steps 🌺 → 🐝 nectar / 🌺 → fruit → seeds
en: A flower helps in two ways: it feeds the bee, and it can turn into a fruit with seeds.
