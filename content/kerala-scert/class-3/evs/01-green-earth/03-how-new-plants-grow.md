# How new plants grow
objective: Know that new plants grow from seeds and from other plant parts.
concept: new-plants
concept.name: How new plants grow
minutes: 6

## say
en: Inside every seed, a baby plant is sleeping. When the seed gets water, it wakes up and starts to grow. This is called germination.
visual: emoji 🫘 💧 🌱

## say
en: Try it. Put a few pea seeds in wet sand near the side of a clear glass. After a few days the root comes out first, going down. Then the shoot comes up and the first leaves open.
visual: steps seed → root grows down → shoot grows up → first leaves

## say
en: A growing plant needs water, air and sunlight, and some manure to grow well. Keep your plant where there is light.
visual: emoji 💧 💨 ☀️

## say
en: New plants do not come only from seeds. A curry leaf plant can send up new plants from its roots. The miracle leaf plant, ilamulachi, grows new plants from its leaves.
visual: emoji 🌿 🍃

## say
label: example
en: Hibiscus and rose can be grown by planting a cut piece of their stem. Mango and pumpkin grow from seeds.
visual: steps stem: hibiscus, rose / root: curry leaf / leaf: ilamulachi / seed: mango, pumpkin

## ask
form: choice
en: When a seed germinates, which part comes out first?
options: the root | the flower | the fruit
answer: the root
hint.en: It grows downwards into the soil.
explain.en: The root comes out first and grows down. Then the shoot grows up.
mistake the flower: thinks flowers come before roots

## ask
form: choice
en: What does a seed need first to start growing?
options: water | salt | darkness and dryness
answer: water
hint.en: Our pea seeds started growing after the sand was made wet.
explain.en: A seed starts to germinate when it gets water.

## ask
form: choice
en: The miracle leaf plant (ilamulachi) makes new plants from which part?
options: leaf | seed | flower
answer: leaf
hint.en: Its name gives you a clue.
explain.en: New ilamulachi plants grow from its leaves.

## ask
form: choice
en: How is a hibiscus plant usually grown?
options: from a piece of stem | from its leaf | from its flower
answer: from a piece of stem
hint.en: Gardeners cut and plant a piece of it.
explain.en: Hibiscus is grown by planting a cut piece of its stem.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which plant grows from a seed?
options: mango | curry leaf root | ilamulachi leaf
answer: mango
hint.en: Think of the big seed inside the fruit we eat.
explain.en: A mango seed grows into a new mango tree.

## ask
form: number
en: A growing plant needs water, air and sunlight. How many things is that?
answer: 3
hint.en: Count: water, air, sunlight.
explain.en: That is 3 things.
mistake 2: missed one of the three

## story
en: Pinky the pea seed was sleeping in dry sand. One day Saira poured water. Pinky stretched! A tiny root wriggled down, a shoot peeped up, and two green leaves waved hello to the sun.

## picture
visual: pattern 🫘 🌱 🌿 🪴
en: A seed grows in steps: first the root, then the shoot, then the leaves.
