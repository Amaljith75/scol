# What is in the soil?
objective: Know what soil contains and why soil matters.
concept: soil
concept.name: Soil and what it contains
minutes: 6

## say
en: Is soil the same everywhere? No! Soil from the courtyard, under a tree and the playground can have different colours and different sizes of grains.
visual: emoji 🟫 🟤 🟧

## say
en: Soil took thousands of years to form, and it is full of life. Earthworms, ants and lakhs of tiny living things live in it.
visual: emoji 🪱 🐜

## say
en: Soil has air in it. Drop a dry clump of soil into water and watch the bubbles come out. Those bubbles are air.
visual: emoji 🟫 💧 🫧

## say
en: Soil has water in it. Close some garden soil in a clear bottle and keep it in the sun. Little drops of water appear on the sides.
visual: emoji 🫙 ☀️ 💧

## say
label: example
en: Soil has biomass too: dried leaves, bits of stems and remains of living things. Plants grow well in soil with lots of biomass, and earthworms eat it.
visual: emoji 🍂 🪱 🌱

## say
en: Plants take water and minerals from the soil, and we use soil for farming and making clay models. So we must keep soil clean and fertile.
visual: emoji 🌾 🏺

## ask
form: choice
en: A dry clump of soil is dropped in water and bubbles come out. What do the bubbles show?
options: there is air in soil | there is sugar in soil | the water is boiling
answer: there is air in soil
hint.en: Bubbles are made of air.
explain.en: The bubbles show that soil has air in it.

## ask
form: choice
en: Soil in a closed bottle is kept in the sun. Drops appear on the sides. What does this show?
options: there is water in soil | the bottle is broken | it rained inside
answer: there is water in soil
hint.en: Where did the drops come from?
explain.en: The drops came from the soil, so soil has water in it.

## ask
form: choice
en: What is biomass in soil?
options: dried leaves and remains of living things | small plastic pieces | stones
answer: dried leaves and remains of living things
hint.en: It comes from plants and animals.
explain.en: Biomass is dried leaves, bits of stems and remains of living things.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which creature eats soil rich in biomass?
options: earthworm | eagle | goat
answer: earthworm
hint.en: It lives in the soil and has no legs.
explain.en: Earthworms feed on soil rich in biomass.

## ask
form: number
en: Soil has air, water and biomass. How many things is that?
answer: 3
hint.en: Count: air, water, biomass.
explain.en: That is 3 things.
mistake 2: missed one of the three

## story
en: Under the mango tree lived Mannu the earthworm. Mannu said, my soil has air to breathe, water to drink, and lots of yummy dried leaves. And when I eat, I make the soil even better for the tree!
