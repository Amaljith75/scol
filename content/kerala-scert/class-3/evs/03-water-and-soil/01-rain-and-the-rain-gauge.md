# Rain and the rain gauge
objective: Measure rainfall with a rain gauge.
concept: rain
concept.name: Rain and measuring rain
minutes: 6

## say
en: Pitter patter! After rain the soil is wet, plants look fresh, and new shoots come up. Rain changes everything around us.
visual: emoji 🌧️ 🌱

## say
en: Some places get a lot of rain and some get very little. How do we know how much rain fell? We measure it.
visual: emoji 🌧️ ☀️

## say
en: A rain gauge measures rain. Make one with a glass bottle, a funnel and a scale. Fix the scale to the bottle, put the funnel on top, and keep it in an open place.
visual: steps bottle + funnel + scale = rain gauge

## say
en: Read the water level in the bottle at the same time every day. The higher the water, the more rain fell that day.
visual: emoji 🧪 📏

## say
label: example
en: Rainwater that falls on soil soaks down into it. Some rainwater also flows away into streams and rivers.
visual: emoji 🌧️ 🟫

## ask
form: choice
en: What do we use to measure rainfall?
options: a rain gauge | a thermometer | a clock
answer: a rain gauge
hint.en: Its name has the word rain in it.
explain.en: A rain gauge measures how much rain fell.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which of these is NOT needed to make our rain gauge?
options: a candle | a funnel | a scale
answer: a candle
hint.en: We used a bottle, a funnel and a scale.
explain.en: We need a glass bottle, a funnel and a scale. Not a candle.

## ask
form: choice
en: On which day did more rain fall: the day the water rose to 5 cm, or the day it rose to 2 cm?
options: 5 cm | 2 cm
answer: 5 cm
hint.en: Higher water means more rain.
explain.en: 5 cm is higher than 2 cm, so more rain fell that day.
mistake 2 cm: thinks a lower water level means more rain

## ask
form: number
en: Our rain gauge showed 3 cm on Monday and 4 cm on Tuesday. How many centimetres of rain on both days together?
answer: 7
hint.en: Add 3 and 4.
explain.en: 3 cm and 4 cm make 7 cm of rain.
mistake 1: subtracted instead of adding

## ask
form: choice
en: Where does rainwater go when it falls on soil?
options: it soaks into the soil | it turns into stones | it goes up into the sky at once
answer: it soaks into the soil
hint.en: Think of our bottle of dry soil.
explain.en: Rainwater soaks down into the soil.

## story
en: Kuttan put a bottle with a funnel in his courtyard. That night it rained and rained. In the morning, the water reached 6 on the scale! Kuttan wrote in his diary: big rain today.

## picture
visual: steps 🧪 2 cm → little rain / 🧪 6 cm → lots of rain
en: More water in the gauge means more rain.
