# Where water comes from
objective: Name water sources and ways to save water.
concept: water-sources
concept.name: Water sources and saving water
minutes: 6

## say
en: Where does the water at your home come from? Many homes have a well. Some have a borewell, a deep narrow hole in the ground. Farms may use ponds.
visual: emoji 🪣 🏞️

## say
en: Rivers, ponds, lakes and wells are all water sources. Rainwater fills them.
visual: emoji 🏞️ 🌧️

## say
en: Kerala has old ways too. A suranga is a narrow tunnel dug into a hill to bring groundwater out. It is used in Kasaragod. A keni is a well made by putting a hollowed tree trunk into the ground. Many tribal groups still use it.
visual: steps suranga → tunnel / keni → tree-trunk well

## say
en: All living things need water. We use it to drink, cook, bathe, wash and farm. Fish live in it, and elephants drink and bathe in it.
visual: emoji 💧 🐟 🐘 🌾

## say
label: example
en: Water is precious, so don't waste it. Close the tap after use, and fix leaking pipes.
visual: emoji 🚰 ✅

## ask
form: choice
en: Which is a water source?
options: a well | a chair | a cupboard
answer: a well
hint.en: We draw water from it with a bucket.
explain.en: A well is a water source.

## ask
form: choice
en: What is a suranga?
options: a tunnel that brings out groundwater | a kind of fish | a big boat
answer: a tunnel that brings out groundwater
hint.en: It is used in Kasaragod.
explain.en: A suranga is a narrow tunnel that brings groundwater out of a hill.

## ask
form: choice
en: What is a keni made from?
options: a hollowed tree trunk | plastic bottles | glass
answer: a hollowed tree trunk
hint.en: It is a well made with wood.
explain.en: A keni is a well made by putting a hollowed tree trunk into the ground.

## ask
form: choice
en: Which of these wastes water?
options: leaving the tap open | closing the tap | fixing a leaking pipe
answer: leaving the tap open
hint.en: Which one lets water run when nobody needs it?
explain.en: Leaving the tap open wastes water. Close it after use.

## ask
form: choice
en: Who needs water?
options: all living things | only fish | only people
answer: all living things
hint.en: Plants, animals and people.
explain.en: All living things need water to live.

## ask
form: number
en: Well, river, chair and pond. How many of these are water sources?
answer: 3
hint.en: A chair does not give water.
explain.en: Well, river and pond: 3 water sources.
mistake 4: counted the chair as a water source

## story
en: A fish in the river called out to Kuttan: please don't throw sweet wrappers in the water. The river is my home, just like your house is yours! Kuttan picked up the wrappers and put them in the bin.
